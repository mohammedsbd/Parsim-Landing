# Executive Summary / ማጠቃለያ

Running a modern school requires much more than teaching. Administrators manage enrollment, student records, attendance, grades, examinations, fees, communication, transportation, staff, reporting, and thousands of daily operational decisions.

When these activities depend on paper files, spreadsheets, separate applications, and phone calls, valuable staff time is lost and important information becomes difficult to track.

**YeneSchool brings these operations together in one integrated platform.**

From enrollment and attendance to finance, examinations, transportation, parent communication, analytics, and AI-powered insights, YeneSchool gives school leadership a single place to manage the institution.

### With YeneSchool, your school can:

* Manage students, teachers, parents, and staff from one platform.
* Automate enrollment and class/section assignment.
* Generate report cards and academic reports automatically.
* Track attendance digitally — including offline operation.
* Manage tuition, fees, discounts, penalties, and payment records.
* Give parents real-time access to grades, attendance, fees, schedules, and announcements.
* Manage school buses, routes, drivers, students, and transportation schedules.
* Send instant notifications to parents and staff.
* Use AI to identify patterns, summarize information, and support school decision-making.
* Generate reports and dashboards for administrators.
* Operate using the Ethiopian calendar.
* Support English, Amharic, Afaan Oromo, Somali, and Arabic.
* Maintain role-based access so every user sees only the information relevant to their responsibilities.

> **Pilot program:** YeneSchool is currently being tested with schools in Addis Ababa. References and demonstrations are available upon request.

# 20. Role-Based Access / የተጠቃሚ ፈቃድ አስተዳደር

Different people need different levels of access.

| Role                  | Main Responsibilities                |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| **Administrator**     | Overall school management            |
| **IT Manager**        | Technical and operational management |
| **Registrar**         | Enrollment and student records       |
| **Teacher**           | Attendance, grades and teaching      |
| **Student**           | Personal academic information        |
| **Parent**            | Child's school information           |
| **Finance**           | Fees, payments and financial reports |
| **Transport Manager** | Buses, routes and transportation     |
| **Driver**            | Assigned transportation activities   |

Users only receive the permissions required for their responsibilities.

For example:

**A teacher does not automatically receive access to financial records.**

**A parent cannot access another student's information.**

This principle helps protect student, family, and school information.
